Who we are

A podcast built to carry Africa's geospatial story to the world

Geospatially Africa started in April 2021 as "Geospatially Podcast" before evolving into a dedicated platform for meaningful conversations about the geospatial industry in Africa and around the world.

Explore Episodes
What we do

Conversations that showcase the continent's geospatial talent

We are Africa's premier platform for geospatial conversations - from GIS and drones to machine learning and beyond. As one of the very few media outlets showcasing Afro-centric geospatial talent, expertise, and innovation on a global stage, we drive the narrative around the immense potential within Africa's geospatial ecosystem.

Our approach

African voices first, open to everyone

We centre African perspectives in the global geospatial conversation, platforming talent, innovation, and expertise from the continent. Every episode is a free resource - we believe open access to industry knowledge accelerates growth for the entire community. It's built by the community, for the community: our guests are nominated by listeners, our topics shaped by what matters most.

Our mission

Driving the African geospatial narrative

Geospatially Africa exists to drive the narrative around the immense potential within Africa's geospatial ecosystem - and how it contributes to the continent's individual and collective development, one conversation at a time.
