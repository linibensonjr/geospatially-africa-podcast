About the show

A platform for Africa's geospatial voices

Geospatially Africa started in April 2021 as "Geospatially Podcast" before evolving into a dedicated platform for meaningful conversations about the geospatial industry in Africa and around the world. As one of the very few media outlets showcasing Afro-centric geospatial talent, we drive the narrative around the continent's spatial potential.
